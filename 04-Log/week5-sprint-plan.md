# Cut Crew — Sprint 2 plan

**Sprint:** weeks 5–6 · Mon 5 Oct → Fri 16 Oct 2026
**Planned:** Mon 5 Oct · **Shown to Luke:** Fri 9 Oct and Fri 16 Oct

## Sprint goal
A clipper pastes a YouTube video, the AI finds the shocking and intriguing moments, and each one comes out with a hook that makes people stay and watch — ready to post.

## My ranked list
1. AI finds the best parts of a long video, so the clipper doesn't have to watch it all
2. Cut out boring bits / dead air
3. Captions
Backlog: strong hook first, viral score, cliffhanger ending, smart reframe, hashtags, many clips in one pass

## The 3 stories

| # | Story | Why it's in |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | **Mark the moment.** As a clipper, I want to save where a clip starts and ends, so I never have to rewatch the video to find it. | The base every clip needs (ranked #1, by hand) |
| 2 | **Ready to post.** As a clipper, I want a hook, caption and hashtags on every clip, so it's ready to post the second it's cut. | The hook is what gets the views (backlog: hook first + hashtags) |
| 3 | **AI moment finder.** As a clipper, I want the AI to read the video and find the shocking, intriguing "wait, what?" moments — with times, a score and 3 hook options — so I don't have to watch the whole thing. | Ranked #1, done by AI. This is what makes people rely on the app |
